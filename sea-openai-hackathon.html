Launched in Singapore on 6 June 2026, the Sea x OpenAI Codex Hackathon marks the start of a first-of-its-kind regional hackathon series, bringing together the best and brightest AI builders from across APAC to create innovative new products that have the potential to shape the experiences of millions.

The visual identity was designed to capture the essence of what this landmark hackathon represented: The power of collaboration, pushing frontiers and endless possibilities in the new age of AI assisted hackathons.

Visual Language

The concentric circle motif fuses the spherical shapes from both Sea and OpenAI logos and uses the palette from Sea to form the individual coloured arcs and spheres.
