The Sea Global Management Associate Program (Sea Global MAP) is Sea's 2-year flagship program for aspiring graduates who seek to create a purposeful impact with technology, and become future leaders of the industry.

Each year the identity refreshes the iconic Sea waves with new energy, welcoming the next wave of Management Associates ready to dive straight into the programme. My work spans between the 2024 to 2026 editions of MAP for the Shopee and Monee track.

Concept

Deconstructing the waves of the Sea logo over 3 years in different ways to show the innovations Management Associates bring and the impact they leave. Reiterated each year, the motif holds to one central idea: every new cohort makes unique waves of their own.
